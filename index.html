About Estora Capital

A disciplined,
performance-driven
approach

We believe sustainable returns are built through discipline, patience, and execution.

Estora Capital is a Lusaka-based real estate investment firm with over 10 years of combined experience across property investment, development, asset management, and operations. We act as a fully integrated partner, managing assets from acquisition through to long-term performance optimization.

Since 2017, we have focused on identifying undervalued opportunities, enhancing asset performance, and delivering sustainable, risk-adjusted returns. Our integrated model ensures control across the full investment lifecycle — from acquisition and development to long-term asset management.
