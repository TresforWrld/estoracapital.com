Who We Are

We don't just invest in property we build it, manage it, and make it perform.

Estora Capital is a Lusaka-based real estate investment firm operating across the full property lifecycle. From identifying undervalued assets to optimizing them for long term income generation, we take a hands-on, disciplined approach at every stage.

Our work is grounded in deep knowledge of the Zambian property market the demand drivers, the development opportunities, and the fundamentals that underpin sustainable returns. We combine that local insight with rigorous investment discipline to deliver results that hold up over time.

"Real estate is not speculation — it is patient capital, disciplined management, and the conviction to build where others see risk."
