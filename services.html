Managing Assets. Creating Value.

We combine development, investment, and management to deliver sustainable real estate performance.

Overview

Integrated. Disciplined.
Performance-driven.

"Our integrated model ensures control across the full investment lifecycle."

From the moment we identify an opportunity through to long-term asset optimization, Estora Capital manages every stage with the same disciplined, hands-on approach that defines our firm.

We have built our service platform to reflect how we believe real estate should be managed — as an active, integrated business, not a passive holding. Each service line reinforces the others, creating compounding operational advantage across the portfolio.

This approach allows us to control costs, improve income performance, and identify value-add opportunities that external managers typically miss.

01

Property Investment

We identify and acquire high-potential assets in strategic locations, focusing on strong fundamentals and long-term growth potential across the Lusaka and broader Zambian market.

Our investment process is grounded in rigorous due diligence, market analysis, and a disciplined approach to pricing. We target assets that can deliver both income yield and capital appreciation.

  • Market research and opportunity identification
  • Acquisition due diligence and structuring
  • Portfolio construction and diversification
  • Risk assessment and investment committee review
Focus Areas
  • Residential rental properties with strong occupancy fundamentals
  • Commercial real estate in high-demand Lusaka corridors
  • Strategic land parcels for future development
  • Mixed-use developments offering income diversification
02

Property Development

We unlock value through land development, renovations, repositioning, and new construction projects that create lasting capital appreciation and expand the income base of the portfolio.

Our development capability means we are not reliant on third parties to execute value-add strategies — we can manage projects directly, controlling costs and timelines.

  • Land development and new construction
  • Renovation and property repositioning
  • Project management and cost control
  • Planning, permitting, and stakeholder management
Development Philosophy

Every development project begins with a clear value creation thesis and ends with a better-performing asset.

03

Asset Management

We actively manage assets to improve income performance, control operational costs, and enhance long-term value through disciplined portfolio oversight and continuous optimization.

Our asset management approach treats each property as a business — setting performance benchmarks, tracking key metrics, and making proactive decisions to protect and grow value.

  • Income performance monitoring and optimization
  • Operational cost control and benchmarking
  • Capital expenditure planning and execution
  • Investor reporting and performance analysis
What We Track
  • Rental income yield vs. market benchmarks
  • Occupancy rates and vacancy cost analysis
  • Operational expenditure and net operating income
  • Capital value and appreciation trajectory
04

Property Management

We ensure efficient day-to-day operations through professional tenant management, structured maintenance coordination, and reliable rent collection — protecting asset quality and income stability.

Strong property management is the foundation of consistent returns. Our hands-on approach reduces vacancy, minimizes maintenance costs, and builds lasting tenant relationships.

  • Tenant sourcing, screening, and onboarding
  • Lease administration and renewal management
  • Maintenance coordination and contractor oversight
  • Rent collection and arrears management
Our Standard

Exceptional property management isn't just about maintenance — it's about protecting the investor's income stream at every touchpoint.
