Sustainability

Responsible investment.
Lasting impact.

We are committed to responsible real estate practices that support long-term value creation — for our investors, our communities, and the broader Zambian economy.

Our Commitment

Beyond returns —
responsible real estate

"Sustainable real estate is not just about environmental standards — it is about how we build, manage, and contribute to the places we operate in."

At Estora Capital, we recognise that our work has consequences beyond the financial. The assets we develop, the tenants we house, and the communities we operate within are all stakeholders in our success. Responsible practice is therefore not a separate agenda — it is woven into how we do business.

We take a practical, long-term view of sustainability. Our approach is not built around external certification or marketing claims — it is built around sound operational decisions that reduce waste, improve quality, and create lasting economic value for Zambia's real estate sector.

We believe that responsible real estate and strong financial returns are not competing goals. When done correctly, they are mutually reinforcing — quality assets in well-managed environments attract quality tenants, command stronger rents, and appreciate more reliably over time.

Responsible stewardship of assets and communities is not a constraint on our investment returns — it is a source of them.

Focus Areas

Where we direct our efforts

Efficient Resource Utilisation

We apply careful resource management across all development and management activities — reducing waste and improving efficiency without compromising quality or performance.

  • Water and energy efficiency in property operations
  • Waste reduction during construction and renovation
  • Preventive maintenance to extend asset lifespan
  • Cost-effective procurement and supply chain management

Sustainable Development

Our development philosophy integrates responsible construction practices and thoughtful land use into every project — building assets that serve their communities for decades.

  • Responsible site selection and land use planning
  • Quality construction standards that reduce lifecycle costs
  • Designs that suit local climate and community context
  • Long-term asset durability over short-term cost savings

Local Economic Growth

We actively support Zambia's real estate sector through investment, employment creation, and the development of high-quality assets that serve and strengthen local communities.

  • Prioritising local contractors and service providers
  • Creating employment through development activity
  • Developing quality assets that elevate local standards
  • Building long-term relationships with community stakeholders
Long-Term Thinking

Sustainability as a business principle

We do not view sustainability as a compliance exercise. We view it as a reflection of the long-term thinking that characterises everything we do at Estora Capital.

The same discipline that drives our investment decisions — patience, quality focus, and attention to long-term consequences — informs how we approach our responsibilities to the environment, our communities, and our stakeholders.

As our portfolio grows, so too will our commitment to raising the standard of responsible real estate practice in Zambia.
